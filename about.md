I am a PhD student at McGill University / Mila supervised by Prof. David Rolnick. I am interested in fundamental learning problems driven by applications of deep neural networks to monitoring planetary change. These include:

- *Geographic distribution shift*: how can we develop models that generalize evenly well to all regions of interest at test time, particularly when some of those regions are under-represented or unseen during training?
- *Learning effective Earth embeddings*: what inductive biases, data sources, and pre-training strategies can yield Earth embeddings that are broadly useful across a wide range of downstream tasks? 
- *Multi-modal fusion*: how can we fuse Earth embeddings with other models to improve predictions on geospatial tasks?

Prior to starting my PhD, I was employed at Microsoft for 5 years - first as a software engineer, then later as an applied scientist. I primarily worked on ML models for satellite imagery and points-of-interest data, from data curation to training to deployment into our production systems.

I grew up in the Greater Toronto Area and currently live in Montréal. If you are interested in my research or any of my blog posts, please do not hesitate to reach out - I would love to hear from you!
